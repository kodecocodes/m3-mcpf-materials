## 💡 Tips for Best Results

- **Be specific**: Use descriptive titles and detailed content
- **Natural language**: Ask in your own words - the MCP understands context
- **Combine operations**: Chain multiple actions in one request
- **Explore the dashboard**: Use "Open my notes dashboard" for visual interaction
- **No search tool yet**: Use `list_notes()` and the dashboard to review notes
